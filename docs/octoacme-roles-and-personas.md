# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Project Sponsor / Executive Sponsor

### Role Summary
Project Sponsors provide strategic direction, funding support, and executive decision-making for key initiatives. They are accountable for ensuring project outcomes align with business priorities.

### Responsibilities
- Confirm strategic objectives, scope boundaries, and success criteria
- Approve major scope, timeline, and funding changes
- Remove organizational blockers escalated by Project Managers
- Align Stakeholders on business priorities and decision outcomes
- Partner with Product Managers on value realization and trade-offs

### Goals
- Ensure initiative outcomes deliver measurable business value
- Maintain executive alignment and timely decision-making
- Keep high-impact work adequately funded and supported

### Typical Communication
- Executive steering updates with Stakeholders and Project Managers
- Decision and escalation checkpoints with Product Managers and Technical Leads
- Priority and business impact reviews with Developers, QA/Testing, and leadership

---

## Business Analyst / Requirements Lead

### Role Summary
Business Analysts translate business and user needs into clear, testable requirements. They create shared understanding between business stakeholders and delivery teams.

### Responsibilities
- Elicit, document, and refine business and user requirements
- Define acceptance criteria and requirement traceability
- Clarify dependencies, assumptions, and edge cases with Project Managers
- Collaborate with Product Managers on prioritization and scope definition
- Support Developers and QA/Testing with requirement clarifications during delivery

### Goals
- Reduce ambiguity in planning and execution
- Improve requirement quality and testability
- Increase delivery predictability by minimizing rework

### Typical Communication
- Requirement workshops with Stakeholders, Product Managers, and Project Managers
- Ongoing clarification threads with Developers and QA/Testing
- Scope and acceptance reviews before sprint or milestone commitments

---

## UX/Product Designer

### Role Summary
UX/Product Designers define user experience, workflows, and interaction patterns to ensure usable, accessible solutions that support product and business goals.

### Responsibilities
- Create user flows, wireframes, prototypes, and design specifications
- Validate usability assumptions with Product Managers and Stakeholders
- Collaborate with Developers on implementation feasibility and constraints
- Work with QA/Testing to define usability and accessibility checks
- Ensure design decisions remain aligned to requirements and project timelines

### Goals
- Deliver intuitive, accessible, and consistent user experiences
- Reduce design-related implementation churn
- Improve user adoption and satisfaction outcomes

### Typical Communication
- Design reviews with Product Managers, Developers, and Stakeholders
- Handoff sessions with Developers and QA/Testing for implementation readiness
- Iteration checkpoints with Project Managers to align design scope and schedule

---

## Technical Lead / Architect

### Role Summary
Technical Leads and Architects guide technical decisions, architecture direction, and engineering standards to keep delivery scalable, secure, and maintainable.

### Responsibilities
- Define solution architecture, technical standards, and integration patterns
- Identify technical risks, dependencies, and non-functional requirements
- Support Developers with design guidance, estimation, and trade-off decisions
- Partner with Project Managers on sequencing and mitigation plans
- Coordinate with Security and Privacy Leads on secure design requirements

### Goals
- Maintain system quality, reliability, and long-term maintainability
- Reduce technical delivery risk and costly rework
- Balance speed of delivery with sound engineering design

### Typical Communication
- Technical planning sessions with Developers and Project Managers
- Architecture trade-off discussions with Product Managers and Stakeholders
- Readiness and risk alignment with QA/Testing, Security, and DevOps/Release roles

---

## Security and Privacy Lead

### Role Summary
Security and Privacy Leads define and enforce security, privacy, and compliance expectations across project delivery and operations.

### Responsibilities
- Define security and privacy requirements for features and data flows
- Conduct risk and threat assessments with Technical Leads and Developers
- Review controls, remediation plans, and secure-release criteria
- Partner with Project Managers to track security risks and escalation paths
- Work with QA/Testing and DevOps/Release roles on validation and deployment controls

### Goals
- Reduce security and privacy risk exposure
- Ensure compliance and policy adherence in delivery decisions
- Improve incident readiness and response coordination

### Typical Communication
- Security review checkpoints with Technical Leads, Developers, and QA/Testing
- Risk and compliance updates to Project Managers, Product Managers, and Sponsors
- Release readiness sign-offs with DevOps/Release and Stakeholders

---

## DevOps / Release or Site Reliability Engineer

### Role Summary
DevOps, Release, and Site Reliability Engineers own delivery pipelines, operational readiness, and service reliability during and after release.

### Responsibilities
- Maintain CI/CD pipelines, release automation, and environment readiness
- Define observability, rollback, and incident response runbooks
- Coordinate release gates with QA/Testing and Developers
- Align release timing and dependency sequencing with Project Managers
- Share operational risk and readiness updates with Stakeholders and Support teams

### Goals
- Deliver stable, predictable, and low-risk releases
- Improve uptime, performance, and recovery outcomes
- Reduce deployment friction and operational toil

### Typical Communication
- Release planning and go/no-go reviews with Project Managers, QA/Testing, and Developers
- Operational readiness updates for Product Managers, Stakeholders, and Sponsors
- Post-release monitoring and incident coordination with Support and Technical Leads

---

## Customer Support / Customer Success Representative

### Role Summary
Customer Support and Customer Success roles bring customer impact signals into planning, delivery, and post-release improvement.

### Responsibilities
- Provide recurring customer pain points and support trend insights
- Prepare support enablement content for upcoming releases
- Validate customer-facing readiness with Product Managers and Project Managers
- Escalate post-release issues to Developers, QA/Testing, and DevOps/Release
- Contribute customer outcome feedback to Stakeholders and Sponsors

### Goals
- Improve customer experience during change and release cycles
- Reduce support load through better readiness and documentation
- Accelerate resolution of customer-impacting issues

### Typical Communication
- Pre-release readiness syncs with Product Managers, Project Managers, and QA/Testing
- Launch and incident communication with DevOps/Release and Developers
- Voice-of-customer updates shared with Stakeholders and Sponsor roles

---

## Data/Analytics Lead

### Role Summary
Data and Analytics Leads define measurement strategy and ensure teams can evaluate whether delivered work achieved expected outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define success metrics, instrumentation requirements, and reporting cadence
- Partner with Product Managers to align KPIs to product outcomes
- Work with Developers and Technical Leads on telemetry implementation
- Support Project Managers with progress and outcome reporting
- Present post-release insights and recommendations to Stakeholders and Sponsors

### Goals
- Enable evidence-based planning and prioritization decisions
- Improve visibility into delivery performance and product impact
- Drive continuous improvement through measurable outcomes

### Typical Communication
- Metric planning workshops with Product Managers, Project Managers, and Developers
- Validation check-ins with QA/Testing for instrumentation and data quality
- Post-release performance reviews with Stakeholders and executive sponsors
